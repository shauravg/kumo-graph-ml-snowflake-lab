02 / Governance

Identity never reaches the model.

✓ Model input: GOVERNED views

customer IDregionchanneljoin dateordersorder linesproducts

Only eligible customers, and only their orders and items.

✗ Excluded: identity table

full nameemail

Separate table, masked, absent from the semantic view and the column allowlist.

Every check above passed live. Wrong account, wrong role, RAW access, unapproved columns or a changed semantic view all stop the run.

04 / Time

Train on the past. Grade on a later month.

Each scoring date gets a fresh snapshot cut at that date, so the model never sees the month it predicts. The model is loaded once and reused.
